KITAGAMES — FULL GAME RULES
===========================

Welcome to KitaGames. These are the rules and controls for every activity in
this site, including the open-world game. All games are free to play in the browser. You can play at your own
pace. The Chill and Cozy activities have no timers, scoreboards, or win
conditions unless a description explicitly says otherwise.

GENERAL SITE RULES
------------------
1. Choose a game card to open it. Use “All games” to return to the library.
2. Use the search field to find a game by name or category.
3. Use the category chips to filter the library. “Favorites” shows games you
   have saved with the heart button.
4. Use “Restart” to restart the current game. Use “Fullscreen” or press F to
   expand the game area. Press Escape or the fullscreen button to leave it.
5. Your personal bests and favorites are saved on this browser. Sign in with
   Google or email to sync those saves through your KitaGames Firebase profile.
   Guest accounts also work, but a guest save is tied to that Firebase guest
   account and the browser/device where it was created unless you link it to a
   permanent Google or email sign-in.
6. High scores are personal records, not prizes. There is no requirement to
   compete, beat another player, or chase a score.
7. Some games use the keyboard; others work with a mouse, touch screen, or both.
   On touch devices, tap the on-screen board or canvas.

ARCADE, PUZZLE, MEMORY, STRATEGY AND SKILL GAMES
-----------------------------------------------

1. NEON SNAKE
Objective: Eat the glowing fruit, grow longer, and avoid crashing.
Controls: Arrow keys or W/A/S/D.
Rules: Each fruit makes the snake longer. The game ends if the snake hits a
wall or runs into its own body. Try to plan turns before the snake reaches them.

2. 2048
Objective: Combine numbered tiles until you create a 2048 tile.
Controls: Arrow keys; on touch screens, swipe in the direction you want to move.
Rules: Every move slides all tiles. Matching values combine into their sum.
After a valid move, a new tile appears. The game ends when no legal move remains.
Keep your largest tile near a corner to make the board easier to manage.

3. TIC TAC TOE
Objective: Make a line of three of your marks.
Controls: Click or tap an empty square.
Rules: Players alternate X and O on a 3×3 board. The first player to make a
horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of three wins. A full board with no line
is a draw. Choose the computer opponent or take turns with another person as
provided by the in-game controls.

4. MEMORY MATCH
Objective: Find all matching pairs of cards.
Controls: Click or tap two cards to reveal them.
Rules: Only two cards can be face-up at once. If they match, they stay revealed.
If they do not match, they turn face-down again. Continue until every pair is
matched. The move count records how many pairs of cards you turned over.

5. MINEFIELD
Objective: Reveal every safe square without revealing a mine.
Controls: Click/tap to reveal. Right-click or long-press to flag a suspected mine.
Rules: A number tells you how many mines touch that square, including diagonals.
Use those clues to work out where mines are located. Revealing a mine ends the
round. Flagging is a note to yourself; it does not remove a mine.

6. BRICK BREAKER
Objective: Clear the bricks by bouncing a ball into them.
Controls: Move the paddle with the mouse, touch, or Left/Right arrow keys.
Rules: Keep the ball above the bottom edge using the paddle. Each collision
with a brick removes that brick. Clear the full layout to finish the round.
You have three lives; losing the ball costs one life.

7. POCKET PONG
Objective: Keep the rally going and reach five points before the computer.
Controls: Move your paddle with the mouse, touch, or W/S keys.
Rules: Return the ball to the other side. Score when the opponent misses.
The computer becomes quicker as a rally develops. The first player to five
points wins the match.

8. FLAPPY ROCKET
Objective: Fly through the gaps without touching an obstacle.
Controls: Space, click, or tap to flap upward.
Rules: Each flap lifts the rocket. Gravity pulls it down between flaps.
Pass through gaps to continue. A collision ends the run; try again to improve
your personal best.

9. BLOCK DROP
Objective: Clear horizontal lines and keep the stack from reaching the top.
Controls: Left/Right arrows move; Up arrow rotates; Down arrow soft-drops;
Space hard-drops the current block.
Rules: Blocks fall into the playfield. Complete a full horizontal line to clear
it. Cleared lines create room and add to your line total. The game ends when a
new block cannot enter the board.

10. SIMON SAYS
Objective: Repeat the growing sequence of colored pads.
Controls: Click or tap the colored pads in the sequence shown.
Rules: Watch the sequence, then repeat it in exactly the same order. Each
successful round adds one more color to remember. One incorrect choice ends
the sequence. Restart to try a new pattern.

11. WHACK-A-MOLE
Objective: Tap the moles as they pop up.
Controls: Click or tap a mole while it is visible.
Rules: The activity lasts 30 seconds. Each successful hit adds one to the hit
count. Moles appear in changing places, so watch the whole garden.

12. REACTION TIME
Objective: Measure how quickly you react to the green signal.
Controls: Press Start, wait for the panel to turn green, then click or tap it.
Rules: Do not click early. An early click is a false start and you must begin
that attempt again. The game measures the time between the green signal and
your click. The best result from the set is saved as your personal best.

13. AIM TRAINER
Objective: Hit as many moving targets as possible.
Controls: Click or tap the target.
Rules: The round lasts 30 seconds. A successful hit adds one to your total and
moves the target to a new position. Keep your pointer ready and make small,
accurate movements.

14. WORD GUESS
Objective: Guess the hidden five-letter word in six attempts.
Controls: Type a five-letter word into the field and press Enter or Submit.
Rules: Green means the letter is correct and in the right place. Gold means the
letter is in the word but in a different place. Gray means the letter is not
in the hidden word. You have six guesses to solve the word.

15. CONNECT FOUR
Objective: Connect four discs in a row before the other player.
Controls: Click or tap the column where you want to drop a disc.
Rules: Two players take turns. Discs fall to the lowest empty space in the
selected column. Connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
A full board without a four-in-a-row is a draw.

16. ROCK PAPER SCISSORS
Objective: Win three rounds before the computer opponent.
Controls: Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors.
Rules: Rock beats Scissors. Scissors beats Paper. Paper beats Rock. Matching
choices are a draw and do not give either player a point. First to three round
wins takes the match.

17. LIGHTS OUT
Objective: Turn every light off.
Controls: Click or tap a light.
Rules: Selecting one light toggles it and its horizontal and vertical neighbors.
A light that is on becomes off; a light that is off becomes on. Continue until
all lights are off. The move counter records each selected light.

18. SLIDE PUZZLE
Objective: Arrange the numbered tiles from 1 to 15 with the empty space last.
Controls: Click or tap a tile next to the empty space.
Rules: Only a tile directly above, below, left, or right of the empty square can
slide into it. Diagonal moves are not allowed. Arrange the numbers in order.

19. MATH SPRINT
Objective: Solve as many arithmetic questions as possible in 30 seconds.
Controls: Type the answer and press Enter or Submit.
Rules: Questions include addition, subtraction, and multiplication. Each
correct answer adds one to the count and brings up another question. The timer
ends the round after 30 seconds.

20. TYPE DASH
Objective: Type the displayed sentence accurately and comfortably.
Controls: Type into the text field. The timer begins with your first keystroke.
Rules: Complete the sentence exactly as shown. The activity calculates words
per minute and accuracy. Correctness matters; there is no need to rush.

CHILL, COZY AND CREATIVE ACTIVITIES
-----------------------------------
These activities are deliberately non-competitive. There are no timers, no
leaderboards, no score requirements, and no losing state. Use them as long as
you like.

21. CLOUD DRIFT
Objective: Make your own peaceful sky.
Controls: Tap/click the sky to add a cloud. Drag a cloud to move it. Use “Add a
cloud” to create one elsewhere, or “Clear sky” to remove all clouds.
Rules: Clouds drift gently on their own. There is no target or finish line.

22. TINY GARDEN
Objective: Plant a pocket garden and help it grow.
Controls: Select a seed type, then tap an empty plot. Press “Water the garden”
to grow every planted seed. Use “Start a fresh patch” to clear the plots.
Rules: You can mix any flowers and herbs. Watering moves a planted seed through
its growth stages. There is no ideal layout and no limit on how long you play.

23. KOI POND
Objective: Enjoy the pond and interact with the koi.
Controls: Tap/click anywhere in the pond to sprinkle food at that spot. Use
“Sprinkle food” to feed the center, and “Pause fish” to pause their movement.
Rules: The fish swim calmly and turn toward food ripples. This is an ambient
activity, not a fishing or score game.

24. RAINY WINDOW
Objective: Set the mood in a cozy rainy room.
Controls: Move the Rain slider to adjust rainfall. Press the light button to
switch between afternoon and evening.
Rules: Watch the rain on the window and adjust the atmosphere. There is no
objective, timer, score, or failure state.

25. STARGAZER
Objective: Connect the stars to make a constellation.
Controls: Choose Little Dipper, Swan, or Star Heart. Tap the glowing stars in
the order requested. “Clear lines” resets the current pattern.
Rules: Find each star in sequence. When all points are connected, the pattern
is complete. You can switch to another constellation or repeat one at any time.

26. COLOR STUDIO
Objective: Draw, doodle, and experiment with color.
Controls: Draw on the canvas with a mouse, finger, or stylus. Select a color,
adjust the Brush slider, choose Eraser, or press Clear canvas.
Rules: The canvas is your own blank space. Make anything you like and change
it whenever you like. There is no objective or score.

ACCESSIBILITY AND FAIR PLAY
---------------------------
- Take breaks whenever you need them.
- Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls are supported where practical.
- Do not use the site to upload or share personal or private information; the
  drawing canvas stays in your browser and is not uploaded to Firestore.
- Your account data is private to your Firebase user ID. Never share your
  password with anyone.
- The site is a collection of small casual activities. Scores are stored as
  personal records only; no public ranking or competition is required.


27. NEON DISTRICT: OPEN CITY
Objective: Explore a large top-down city with moving traffic and sidewalk
pedestrians, borrow cars, complete delivery jobs, collect cash bundles, visit
the garage and try to shake police pursuit.
Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys to move/drive; E to enter/exit/interact; Shift to
sprint or boost; Space to handbrake; P to pause; F for fullscreen. On phones,
use the on-screen D-pad and action buttons.
Rules: Green markers show job contacts; reach one and press E to accept the job.
A pink marker then shows the delivery destination; reach it and press E to
collect the reward. Enter a parked vehicle by standing next to it and pressing
E; doing so raises your wanted level. Avoid nearby police and stay out of sight
until the wanted level cools down. The blue Garage marker repairs your active
vehicle for $75. Yellow cash bundles can be collected while exploring. If your
health runs out, you pay $150 bail and respawn downtown. Cash and completed jobs
are saved locally in the current browser. See NEON_DISTRICT_RULES.txt for the
full player guide.
