NEON DISTRICT: OPEN CITY — PLAYER GUIDE
=======================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
Neon District is an original, top-down open-world city sandbox made for
KitaGames. It is a small arcade-style city adventure, not a 3D recreation of
any existing game. The city is generated in Canvas and all play runs locally
in your browser.

CONTROLS
--------
WASD or Arrow Keys : Walk on foot / steer and drive a car
E                   : Enter or exit a parked car; accept a job; deliver a job
Shift               : Sprint on foot / boost while driving
Space               : Handbrake while driving
P                   : Pause or resume
F                   : Fullscreen (same as the KitaGames fullscreen button)
Touch controls     : On-screen D-pad, Boost, Use and Brake buttons

GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Look at the minimap in the top-right corner. The green marker is the next
   job contact; the pink marker is the delivery drop after you accept a job.
2. Walk or drive to the green marker. Get close and press E / USE.
3. Follow the pink marker to the destination and press E / USE to finish.
4. Earn cash for each completed delivery. Jobs rotate through the city.
5. Look for parked cars. Get close and press E to borrow a ride. This raises
   your wanted level, so expect a police chase.
6. Find the blue GARAGE marker and press E while close to repair your car for
   $75. You must be in a vehicle to use garage repairs.
7. Collect small yellow cash bundles as you explore. The minimap shows your
   position, the active job, parked cars and police pursuit markers.

WANTED LEVEL
------------
Borrowing a parked vehicle attracts attention. Driving extremely fast can
also draw police attention. Police vehicles pursue you. Break away and stay
out of their nearby search area; after a while, wanted stars cool down. If
your health reaches zero, you are picked up, pay a $150 bail fee, and return
to downtown.

SAVE DATA
---------
Cash and completed jobs are saved in this browser's local storage. This is
not a server save and will not automatically follow you to another device.
Clearing site data can remove this local progress.

NOTES
-----
- This is an original 2D top-down city game designed to be playable in a web
  page without commands, downloads or a custom backend.
- The minimap shows the street grid, your position, and the next job marker.
- The game can be restarted with KitaGames' Restart button.
- On small screens use the on-screen controls; fullscreen gives more room.
